Colour tigers and other wildlife in 100 illustrated pages for iPhone and iPad. Browse the collection by thumbnail, search by title or subtitle, or filter the collection to show only tigers. The featured button reopens the last page you viewed, and My pages gathers your photo pages and pages you’ve started colouring.

Choose from 14 swatches and tap an outlined region to fill it. Use the Paper swatch to remove colour from a region, undo or redo edits, and switch between a full-page view and a scrollable zoomed view. Save your artwork as a PNG to Files or Photos.

Choose a picture from your photo library or take one when a camera is available. Tiger Trails turns the light and dark areas of the picture into an outlined page you can colour in the same studio. Your edits are saved on the device so you can carry on later.

The interface is in English.
