Color tigers and other wildlife in 100 illustrated pages for iPhone and iPad. Browse thumbnails, search by title or subtitle, or filter the collection to show only tigers. The featured button takes you back to the last page you viewed. Open My pages to find pages you made from photos and pages you’ve started coloring.

Select one of 14 swatches and tap an outlined region to fill it. Use the Paper swatch to remove color from a region, undo or redo edits, or switch between a full-page view and a scrollable zoomed view. Export your artwork as a PNG to Files or Photos.

Pick a picture from your photo library or take one when a camera is available. Tiger Trails uses the light and dark areas of the picture to create an outlined page you can color in the same studio. Edits are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off.

The interface is in English.
