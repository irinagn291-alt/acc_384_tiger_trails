The app opens directly to the collection with 100 uncoloured pages on a fresh install. The interface is in English. No account is required. Tap the featured button to open Bengal tiger. Select a swatch and tap an outlined region, then try Undo, Redo and the Paper swatch to remove a fill. Leave a region coloured, go back to the collection and reopen the page from My pages to verify that the fill persists.

To test photo conversion, add an image to the simulator’s photo library, tap the collection’s photo button and select the image in the system photo picker. The converted page opens in the studio and appears in My pages. A camera is optional. The app requests camera access when you choose the camera option and add-only Photos access when you save a PNG to Photos. Saving to Files uses the system exporter. Edit histories and converted pages are stored locally. The app has no analytics, advertising or remote service integrations.
